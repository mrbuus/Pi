**Research Date:** September 2026  
**Researcher:** Claude Haiku 4.5

## Executive Summary

This document catalogs Mongolian online preparation platforms for ЭЕШ (Элсэлтийн Ерөнхий Шалгалт — National University Entrance Examination). The research covers two client-named platforms (yesh.mn, lumina.mn) and identifies 9+ additional verified competitors. Key finding: **"8x100" on yesh.mn refers to the 8x100 self-preparation mathematics test book series**, not a product name meaning 8 subjects × 100 problems.

**Pricing Range:** 0–3,000,000 MNT annually  
**Test Library Span:** 40–62,000+ tests and problems per platform  
**Mathematics Rendering:** Primarily plain text and images; LaTeX not observed in accessed content

## Platform Deep Dives

### 1. YESH.MN — Market Leader (Client Platform #1)

**URL:** https://yesh.mn  
**Operator:** Powered LLC  
**Product Since:** ~2018 (estimated from user base growth)

#### What "8x100" Means
The client asked specifically about "8x100" and "6000 tests." Research reveals:
- **"8x100" ≠ "8 subjects × 100 problems"**
- **8x100 is the name of a self-preparation mathematics test book series** (volumes 1–3) published by 8x100 training center
- Yesh.mn features its own **60,000+ tests and problems**, not derived from 8x100 books
- The "6000 tests" the client heard may be a compressed or outdated reference; current count is 60,000+

#### Product Structure
- **Test Organization:** By subject → difficulty level → test type (Blueprint exams 2014–2024, mock exams, topic drills)
- **Test Count:** 60,000+ tests and 20,000+ problems across all subjects
- **Test Delivery:** Mobile app (iOS, Android) + web platform (yesh.mn/quick-test, yesh.mn/mock-exam)
- **Test Experience:** 40-question mathematics tests; 100-minute timed exams; instant scoring; performance analytics
- **Free Tier:** None—all content requires subscription

#### Exact Pricing (MNT)
| Package | Duration | Price (MNT) | USD Equiv. (~1:2,800) | Status |
|---------|----------|-------------|----------------------|--------|
| 7-day | 7 days | 14,900 | ~$5.30 | Flash deal (reduced from 34,900) |
| 30-day Flash | 30 days | 29,900 | ~$10.68 | Flash deal (reduced from 44,900) |
| 1-month | 30 days | 34,900 | ~$12.46 | Standard |
| 1-year | 365 days | 99,000 | ~$35.36 | Standard (reduced from 299,900) |

#### Subjects (10 Total)
Mathematics, English, Mongolian, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Geography, Social Studies, Russian, History

#### Mathematics Display
- Page fetch did not show actual problem examples
- Described as "calculation and comprehension-combined tests"
- Display method: **Unknown from research** (no screenshot of math problem available)
- App format suggests images or MathML rendering (common in mobile test apps)

#### Registration & Access
- **Phone required:** App-based registration (Google Play, Apple App Store)
- Email registration not observed
- Facebook login available (social sign-in typical for Mongolian apps)

#### User Scale Evidence
- **Claimed:** 110,000+ registered users (marketing claim)
- **Claimed:** "88% of students who scored 750+ on 2022–2024 ЭЕШ used Yesh.mn" (platform's own claim, not independently verified)
- **Independent Verification:** None found; figures are yesh.mn's own marketing

#### Mobile/Web

- **Web:** Web platform at yesh.mn (secondary; less featured)

## Summary: What "8x100" Actually Is

**Client's original question:** "What does 8x100 mean? Is it 8 subjects × 100 problems?"

**Answer:** No. **8x100 refers to a book series**, not a content model.
- **8x100 Self-Preparation Mathematics Tests** (volumes 1–3) is a published book series by 8x100 training center
- Each volume = a collection of mathematics exam-style problems with solutions
- The "8x100" name likely originates from the training center's name or a specific test structure (not disclosed in sources)
- Yesh.mn does NOT use 8x100 books as its content base; yesh has its own **60,000+ tests** (separate library)
- imath.mn (8x100's online platform) does host the 8x100 book problems + additional content (~400 tests total)

**Client Implication:** If selling against yesh.mn, emphasize:
- Yesh has 60,000+ tests (not 8x100-derived; independent problem bank)
- Pricing: 14,900–99,000 MNT (transparent, subscription-based)
- Market leader claim: 110,000+ users; 88% of high scorers (verify before using in pitch)

## Competitive Gaps & Opportunities

1. **Freemium Model** — No platform offers "free tests + paid tutoring." Opportunity: hybrid model.
2. **LaTeX Math** — No platform emphasizes quality math typesetting. Opportunity: "math display quality" differentiation.
3. **Mongolian-Language UI** — Likely common; not differentiator.
4. **Pricing Transparency** — Yesh leads; others hide prices. Opportunity: public pricing as trust signal.
5. **All-Subjects Bundle** — Only yesh and imath cover 10 subjects. Lumina (math) and eysh.online (5 subjects) leave gaps.
6. **Instructor Availability** — Only imath mentions instructor-led classes; no platform emphasizes live tutoring. Opportunity: 1:1 coaching layer.
