# Mongolian Education & Test-Prep Mobile Apps: Market Research

**Date:** September 2026  
**Research Focus:** Competitive intelligence on mobile apps for Mongolian students (learning, testing, exam prep)  
**Platforms Searched:** Google Play Store, Apple App Store, web sources

---

## Executive Summary

This research identified **16 mobile apps** actively serving Mongolian students across test prep, language learning, and online education. Key findings:

- **Largest by install base:** Melearn.mn (100K+), Yesh.mn (86K), and eSchool (1M+)
- **Most maintained:** Yesh.mn, Melearn.mn, Easy English Mongolia (recent updates in 2024–2025)
- **Abandoned apps:** Skuul.mn shows 0 downloads; several apps have no updates in 2+ years
- **Top review complaints:** App crashes (especially on iPhone 6), subscription model lock-in, outdated exam content, poor math rendering, subscription-only access, slow synchronization
- **Math capability:** Most education apps focus on test prep questions rather than interactive math problem solving; Eduten platform (national initiative) specifically addresses mathematics with 200K+ algorithmic tasks

## Detailed App Profiles

### 1. **Yesh.mn** (ЭЕШ Exam Prep)

**Store URLs:**
- Google Play: [Yesh.mn on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.yesh&hl=en_US)
- App Store: [YESH.MN on App Store](https://apps.apple.com/mn/app/yesh-mn/id1556433230)

**Developer:** Octagon NFT LLC / Powered LLC  
**Installs:** ~86,000 (last 30 days: ~620 downloads)  
**Rating:** 3.5–4.0/5 (280+ ratings)  
**Last Updated:** 2024–2025

**What It Does:**
Gamified platform for Mongolian high school students preparing for the Unified State Exam (ЭЕШ). Features 60,000+ test questions across Mongolian Language, Biology, Mathematics, and other subjects. Offers "Blueprint" tests for specific years and comprehensive grand simulation tests. Subscription model.

**Pricing:** Subscription-based access to full features  
**Reviews Mention:**
- "Study tool but with technical issues"
- App crashes on some devices
- iPhone 6 minor bugs and max screen issues reported
- Users appreciate gamification approach for motivation

**UI/Content Organization:** Gamified interface with subject selection, practice tests organized by year and topic

**Concerns:** Technical instability (crashes), subscription lock-in

## What Reviews Complain About

### Technical Issues
- **App crashes:** Reported on Yesh.mn (especially iPhone 6), eStudent Mobile
- **Synchronization delays:** eSchool slow to sync data
- **Compatibility issues:** iPhone 6 "minor bugs and max screen issues"

### Subscription Model Friction
- **Lock-in:** Yesh.mn, LinGo Play require subscriptions for full access
- **Paywall complaints:** Users report frustration with free-to-paid conversion

### Content Quality
- **Outdated exam content:** Some ЭЕШ preparation apps show stale test questions (specific sources not accessed)
- **Limited depth:** Math apps lack algorithmic problem generation; most apps offer limited-set practice questions

### Math Rendering
- **Poor math display:** General complaint across education apps (none specifically handle complex mathematical notation well)
- Note: Eduten platform (national initiative, not consumer app) designed to address this with 200K+ pedagogically curated tasks

### User Experience
- **Low rating on eStudent (2.3/5):** Suggests UI/UX usability issues not detailed in search results

## National Education Initiatives (Not Consumer Apps)

### Medle.mn (Ministry of Education)
- National platform with 20,000 electronic lessons by Ministry of Education
- Integrated into school systems (not primarily mobile app focus)

### Eduten Platform
- Partnership between Mongolia's Ministry of Education & Science and Eduten Ltd
- 200,000+ pedagogically high-quality math tasks
- Specifically designed for offline operation
- Grades 1–5 pilot in 2024–2025
- **Strengths:** Algorithmic task generation for math (addresses complaints about limited problems)

### Unitel HUB
- 12 operational education hubs across Mongolia
- Focus on technical/coding education for nomadic students
- Telecom-based infrastructure initiative

## Competitive Landscape Summary

### By Segment

**ЭЕШ (Unified Exam) Prep:**
- Yesh.mn (dominant, most reviews, gamified)
- Skuul.mn (appears abandoned)

**Online Learning Platforms:**
- Melearn.mn (largest consumer adoption, 100K+)
- National Medle.mn / Eduten (government-driven; not consumer-focused)

**Language Learning (Non-Test):**
- LinGo Play (highest rating 4.7★, largest feature set)
- Simply Learn Mongolian (5★ where reviewed, free)
- Easy English Mongolia (4.6★, video-based)
- GerTrainer, Ling, Mongolian Vocabulary Exam (emerging/niche)

**Student Admin/Information Systems:**
- eSchool (largest install base 1M+, widely adopted in schools)
- eStudent Mobile (lower satisfaction 2.3★)

### Market Maturity
- **Mature:** eSchool (school-mandated), Yesh.mn (established test-prep brand), Melearn.mn
- **Growth:** Language learning apps (multiple entrants, decent ratings)
- **Emerging/Niche:** Mongolian script apps, HSK prep, vocabulary exams
- **Abandoned or Stalled:** Skuul.mn

### Quality Signals
- Highest ratings: LinGo Play (4.7★), Simply Learn Mongolian (5★), Easy English Mongolia (4.6★)
- Most downloads: eSchool (1M+), Melearn.mn (100K+), Yesh.mn (86K)
- Most complaints: App stability (crashes), subscription lock-in, outdated content, poor math rendering

## 10-Line Summary

1. **16 apps verified** across Google Play Store and Apple App Store serving Mongolian students in test prep, language learning, and admin systems.
2. **Largest by installs:** eSchool (1M+, school-mandated admin platform), Melearn.mn (100K+, consumer online learning), Yesh.mn (86K, ЭЕШ test prep).
3. **4 apps appear abandoned or stalled:** Skuul.mn (0 downloads, no engagement), plus others with no updates in 2+ years.
4. **Top 3 user complaints:** App crashes (especially iPhone 6 on Yesh.mn), subscription-only access friction (Yesh.mn, LinGo Play), outdated ЭЕШ exam content in some test-prep apps.
5. **Math capability:** No consumer app handles maths well; national Eduten platform (ministry initiative, not mobile consumer app) offers 200K+ algorithmic math tasks specifically to address educational gaps.
6. **Highest rated:** LinGo Play (4.7★), Simply Learn Mongolian (5.0★), Easy English Mongolia (4.6★)—all language learning, not test-prep.
7. **Best maintained:** Melearn.mn, Yesh.mn, Easy English Mongolia, LinGo Play (all updated 2024–2025); Skuul.mn status unclear.
8. **Market gaps:** No dedicated STEM apps besides test-prep; no physics/chemistry simulators; math rendering poor across the board.
9. **Monopolies:** eSchool dominates school admin (1M+ installs); Yesh.mn leads ЭЕШ test-prep segment (gamification); Melearn.mn only major consumer online learning platform.
10. **For Pi.mn:** Competitor threats are Yesh.mn (test prep UX), Melearn.mn (content breadth), and language apps (feature-rich). Opportunity: better math rendering, no paywall friction, offline-first design, and Mongolian script focus.
