## 1. Mathematics Exam Structure

### Exam Duration
- **Mathematics duration:** 100 minutes (same block as Physics and Chemistry, but subject is separate)
- Other subjects: 80 minutes

**Source:** [2025 оны Элсэлтийн Шалгалтын ХУВААРЬ - iKon.mn](https://ikon.mn/n/3clc)

### Question Types & Format

| Component | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Multiple Choice Section** | 36 questions, 5 answer choices (A–E), **72 points** |
| **Total Sections** | 10 sections across the exam |
| **Answer Types** | Multiple choice + numeric fill-in (open-ended numeric answers) |
| **Question Pool** | 2025: Drawn from 1000 publicly released practice problems (first year using open-source problem bank) |

**Source:** [WebSearch: ЭЕШ математикийн даалгавар structure](https://www.eec.mn/post/6216); [Empasoft Institute announcement](https://empasoft.ider.edu.mn/2025-%D0%BE%D0%BD%D1%8B-%D1%8D%D0%B5%D1%88-%D0%BC%D0%B0%D1%82%D0%B5%D0%BC%D0%B0%D1%82%D0%B8%D0%BA%D0%B8%D0%B9%D0%BD-%D1%85%D0%B8%D1%87%D1%8D%D1%8D%D0%BB%D0%B8%D0%BD-%D0%BD/)

### Exam Versions
Four parallel versions released annually:
- Version A (ЭШ-2025 Математик A хувилбар)
- Version B (ЭШ-2025 Математик B хувилбар)
- Version C (ЭШ-2025 Математик C хувилбар)

## 2. Scoring System

### Scoring Metrics (4-dimensional assessment)

| Metric | Scale | Description |
|--------|-------|-------------|
| **Raw Score** (Анхны оноо) | 0–100 | Percentage of questions answered correctly on the exam |
| **Scaled Score** (Хэмжээст оноо) | 200–800 | Norm-referenced score adjusted against all test-takers' performance; mean 500, SD 100 |
| **Percentile Score** (Проценталь оноо) | 1–99 | Percentage of test-takers scoring below this student |
| **Quality Index** (Амжилтын чанарын индекс) | A–F (9 levels) | Letter grade based on national average performance |

### Scaling Methodology
- **Mean:** 500 points
- **Standard Deviation:** 100 points
- Uses **normal distribution** to normalize across years and versions
- Raw score is the primary input; scaled score adjusted by comparing student performance to all test-takers on the same exam version

**Source:** [БҮТ: ЭЕШ-ын дүн оноог тооцоолох аргачлал - ubn.mn](https://ubn.mn/p/4591); [EEC.mn: ЭЕШ-ЫН ҮНЭЛГЭЭ, ОНОО](https://eec.mn/post/935)

## 4. Official Mathematics Curriculum (Syllabus)

### Curriculum Structure
Mongolia uses a **two-track mathematics curriculum for secondary schools:**

1. **Mathematics-I (Математик-I)** 
   - Mandatory content (заавал судлах)
   - For students taking mathematics as a support subject
   - Tested on ЭЕШ for students in this track

2. **Mathematics-II (Математик-II)** 
   - Optional/elective content (сонгон судлах)
   - For students pursuing higher education in math-heavy fields (engineering, science, economics)
   - Tested on ЭЕШ for students in this track
   - Covers advanced topics beyond core curriculum

### Typical Topic Coverage (based on exam structure)
The exam structure suggests coverage across 10 sections including but not limited to:
- Algebra and equations
- Geometry and trigonometry
- Functions and calculus
- Probability and statistics
- Numerical problem-solving

## 8. Recent or Announced Changes

### 2025: Major Innovation

**Open Problem Bank Introduction**
- First year: ЭЕШ mathematics exam drawn **directly from publicly released pool of 1000 problems**
- Rationale: Increase transparency, reduce test anxiety, allow student preparation from official source
- All 1000 problems published before exam administration
- Impact: Students can theoretically practice with exact problem types and difficulty distribution

**Possible Scoring System Adjustment (Unconfirmed)**
- 2024 math scores dropped significantly (raw mean 24, down from 27); some commentary suggests exam difficulty increased
- No official announcement of methodology change; likely reflects harder question set

**Mandatory Five-Subject Requirement (Since 2024)**
- As of 2024, all ЭЕШ test-takers must take **five subjects, including:**
  - Mathematics (core)
  - Mongolian Language (core)
  - English Language (core)
  - Two of: Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Geography, History, Social Studies
- Allows some selection but makes mathematics universal requirement

**Source:** [news.zindaa.mn: 2024 оноос ЭЕШ-д дараах ТАВАН хичээлээр ЗААВАЛ шалгалт авна](https://news.zindaa.mn/490r); [Empasoft Institute: 2025 Mathematics announcement](https://empasoft.ider.edu.mn/2025-%D0%BE%D0%BD%D1%8B-%D1%8D%D0%B5%D1%88-%D0%BC%D0%B0%D1%82%D0%B5%D0%BC%D0%B0%D1%82%D0%B8%D0%BA%D0%B8%D0%B9%D0%BD-%D1%85%D0%B8%D1%87%D1%8D%D1%8D%D0%BB%D0%B8%D0%BD-%D0%BD/)

## 10. Market Sizing Summary: Is 30,000 Registered Users Realistic?

### Addressable Market Analysis

**Annual Mathematics Test-Taker Cohort:**
- 2024: 23,002 students took mathematics
- 2025 estimated: ~21,500–22,000 (based on 53% math selection rate among 43,318 total test-takers)
- Annual recurring: **21,000–23,000 students per year** take ЭЕШ mathematics

**30,000 User Target Evaluation:**

| Scenario | Assessment |
|----------|-----------|
| **Conservative (2024 baseline)** | 30,000 = 130% of annual math test-takers → **Exceeds 1-year cohort, requires multi-year accumulation or broader audience** |
| **Optimistic (growth + non-test-takers)** | If including repeated sitters + parents/teachers + preparation-phase users not yet sitting exam: plausible over 18–24 months |
| **Realistic (12-month target)** | **~18,000–20,000 active learners** from annual cohort = reasonable; 30,000 would require: (a) growth in test-taker volume, (b) inclusion of non-exam-takers, or (c) 18-month runway |

### Recommendation
- **30,000 registered users is ambitious but NOT impossible** if:
  1. Platform launches 12–18 months before June 2026 exam (captures prep phase)
  2. Includes free-tier users (non-paying registrants for practice)
  3. Targets both exam-takers and younger secondary students preparing years ahead
  4. Accounts for parent/tutor accounts and household penetration

- **More realistic 12-month target: 18,000–22,000 active prep users** aligned with annual math test-taker cohort
