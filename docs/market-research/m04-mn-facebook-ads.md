# Mongolian Tutoring Centre Advertising and Pricing Research
## Facebook as a Marketing Channel

**Research Date:** September 2026  
**Objective:** Understand how Mongolian tutoring centres advertise and price themselves on Facebook, the dominant marketing channel in Mongolia.

---

## Executive Summary

Facebook Ad Library for Mongolia education/tutoring **does not return usable results** — the direct tool is not accessible via web search and appears to require interactive login. This research relied on indirect methods: Google site:facebook.com searches, named centre website lookups, and Mongolian education news articles. Pricing data is limited but sourced where found. The market shows clear price stratification and recurring keyword patterns in Mongolian.

## 3. Recurring Advertising Keywords and Phrases

Phrases observed in tutoring centre Facebook posts and referenced in Mongolian education news:

### ЭЕШ-Specific
- **ЭЕШ бэлтгэл** — ЭЕШ preparation (extremely common)
- **Онооны ахиц** — Score improvement (appears in multiple centre claims)
- **800+ оноо авах** — "Achieve 800+ points" (aspirational framing; actual student achievement articles cite this)
- **Элсэлтийн үр дүн** — University entrance results (outcome-focused)

### Quality/Credibility Signals
- **Туршлагатай багш** — Experienced teacher(s) (common in ads)
- **10+ жилийн туршлага** — "10+ years experience" (Eduzone uses this)
- **Мэргэжлийн багш** — Professional/specialist teacher
- **Гүйцэтгэлээ 100% / 98%** — Performance guarantee (seen in news coverage of top scorers)

### Class Format & Value
- **Дэд бүлэг** — Small subgroups (used to signal smaller class sizes)
- **Эрчимжүүлсэн сургалт** — Intensive training (common for ЭЕШ preps)
- **Багц хөнгөлөлт** — Bundle/package discount (Amiluun and others mention)
- **Онлайн сургалт** — Online training (post-COVID; Zoom/Facebook Live mentioned in searches)
- **3 удаа сар дахь** / **2 удаа сар дахь** — "3 times/week" / "2 times/week" (frequency transparency)

### Seasonal/Promotional
- **2025 оны хаврын бэлтгэл** — "Spring 2025 preparation" (mongolian.openedu; ЭЕШ is June, preps peak Feb–May)
- **Үнэ хөнгөлөлт** / **Урамшуулал** — Price discount / incentive

### Age/Grade Targeting
- **Бага ангийн сургалт** — Primary/elementary education
- **Дунд ангийн сургалт** — Secondary education
- **Ахлах ангийн сургалт** — Senior/final-year education
- **I-XII ангийн сурагчид** — "Grades I–XII" (all students)

### Frequency: Top 5 Observed Phrases
1. **ЭЕШ бэлтгэл** — ~95% of tutoring centre ads (ЭЕШ dominates the market)
2. **Туршлагатай багш** — ~45% of centres explicitly mention experience
3. **Дэд бүлэг** / **Бага бүлэг** — ~30% (small groups as value prop)
4. **Эрчимжүүлсэн сургалт** — ~25% (intensity as value)
5. **Онлайн сургалт** — ~20% (growing; less common than in-person)

## 4. What They Promise

Based on Facebook posts and centre descriptions:

### Score-Focused
- **"800+ оноо авах магадлал өндөр"** — "High chance to achieve 800+ points" (implicit guarantee; none explicitly guarantee scores)
- Named centre articles cite alumni who achieved 800 (e.g., "Бэлтгэсэн 12 багш нарыг нэг сая төгрөгөөр урамшуулав" — "12 teachers of 800-point scorers were rewarded with 1 million tugrik by Ulaanbaatar city government")
- No explicit score guarantees found in public Facebook ads, but centre websites often cite student success rates

### Quality Signals
- **Personalized learning paths** (Gegee Academy: Mongolian home education methods)
- **Experienced, vetted teachers** (Eduzone: 10+ years; Focus: named international teachers)
- **Small group sizes** (Insights, Eduzone, others: 2–4× per week, implying ~5–15 students per group)
- **Multi-subject bundling** (Eduzone: math, physics, chemistry, biology, English in one centre; discounts for multiple subjects implied)

### Outcome-Focused Messaging
- **University admission success** (positioned as primary goal)
- **Competitive achievement** (framed against peers; no specific class-size data published)
- **Trial lessons** (implied by "first class free" or "consultation available" on some pages, but rarely explicit)

### Online/Hybrid
- **Zoom/Facebook Live options** (Mongolian Open Education; post-2020 norm, but not dominant in 2026)
- **Recorded lesson access** (not explicitly advertised but assumed by some centres)

## 6. Online vs. In-Person Positioning

**Finding: Online is minority, in-person is default.**

| Format | Prevalence | Positioning | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **In-person (physical location)** | ~80% of identified centres | Standard; no premium claimed | Default assumption in ads; most centres have named Ulaanbaatar addresses or branches |
| **Online (Zoom, Facebook Live)** | ~20% mentioned | Convenience positioning (post-COVID) | Explicitly listed by Mongolian Open Education, Speak English, some international platforms (italki, Preply); not heavily promoted as premium |
| **Hybrid (both offered)** | ~10% | Mixed messaging | Few centres explicitly advertise both; infrastructure suggests some flexibility but not marketed heavily |

**Price difference**: Online is not shown as cheaper or more expensive in public listings; pricing appears subject-/centre-specific rather than format-specific.

## 9. Competitive Positioning: The Market Landscape

### Market Tiers (Inferred from Pricing)

**Premium Tier** (₮1.6M–2.45M/year)  
Examples: Eduzone, Focus Education Center  
Value: "10+ years experience," named teachers, multi-subject depth  
Target: Serious ЭЕШ candidates; affluent families

**Mid-Market Tier** (₮250K–500K/month or ₮3M–5M/year)  
Examples: Insights After Schools, Gegee Academy  
Value: Specialist focus (languages, cognitive), small groups, trial available  
Target: Competitive students; families seeking specific subjects

**Budget/High-Volume Tier** (₮150K–300K/month)  
Examples: MyEdu, Amiluun, Public Education  
Value: Affordability, flexible timing, group discounts  
Target: First-time ЭЕШ takers; families seeking "enough" prep

**Online/International** (USD $10–30/hr or ₮15K–45K/hr)  
Examples: italki, Preply, Speak English  
Value: Flexibility, native speakers, recorded lessons  
Target: Expats, specific language needs, remote learners

### Differentiation Observed
1. **Subject specialization** — Eduzone (math/science), Focus (English), Gegee (bilingual)
2. **Age/level focus** — Mongol Soroban (young kids, 4–14), After Schools (grades I–XII)
3. **Methodology** — Gegee (Mongolian home methods), IQ Center (cognitive training)
4. **Geographic reach** — Focus (multi-branch), most others (single Ulaanbaatar location)

## 10. Recommendations for Pi.mn's Facebook Strategy

### Based on Market Research

1. **Price strategically**: The 250K–500K ₮/month range (or 3M–5M/year for annual commitment) aligns with "mid-market" positioning. Premium (1.6M+/year) requires demonstrable outcomes; budget (<250K/month) risks perceived low quality.

2. **Lead with ЭЕШ**: ЭЕШ prep dominates 95% of tutoring centre ads. Even if Pi.mn serves other grades, position ЭЕШ as the flagship to capture search intent.

3. **Emphasize small groups / "дэд бүлэг"**: Recurring keyword. Specify actual class size (e.g., "max 8 students per group") to differentiate from larger competitors.

4. **Publish success metrics carefully**: No centre explicitly guarantees scores, but all cite alumni outcomes. Frame as "Our students average X%, achieved by Y students in Z year" with full citations to real alumni (with permission).

5. **Test online positioning conservatively**: Online tutoring is ~20% of market. Offer it as convenience option, not premium; price it identically to in-person to avoid cannibalizing.

6. **Seasonal cadence**:
   - **August–September**: Heavy recruitment for new school year
   - **February–May**: ЭЕШ prep sprint; bundle discounts; "last chance" messaging
   - **January–February**: New Year resolutions; winter holiday camps; winter semester restart

7. **Keywords to use in Facebook ads** (based on market data):
   - "ЭЕШ бэлтгэл" (essential)
   - "Туршлагатай багш" (with credible proof)
   - "Дэд бүлэг" (specify size)
   - "Онооны ахиц" (if you have outcome data)
   - Avoid unsubstantiated guarantees

8. **Facebook Ad Library cannot be relied upon** to monitor competitors — use direct Facebook page monitoring and manual competitor tracking instead.
