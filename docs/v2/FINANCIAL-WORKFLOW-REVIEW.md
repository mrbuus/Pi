# Financial workflow review

Only the isolated pi.mnv2 workspace and guarded PostgreSQL 127.0.0.1:5544/pimn_v2 were used. Fixtures contain synthetic users, products, bank rows and ledger statuses. No provider, external transfer, production database, real statement or private financial record was used. A synthetic CONFIRMED/PAID fixture is not evidence of money movement.

## Concrete fixes

- Store purchase previously trusted any own confirmed payment regardless of amount, tuition month or prior consumption. It now checks ownership, sufficient amount, non-reversed status, no tuition-month designation and no existing product/pass use. It serializes account/product retries and locks payment rows against cross-product reuse. An eligible confirmed payment upgrades the same pending purchase.
- Purchases without confirmed settlement retain no entitlement. Active ownership is returned on retry. Prices come from the server. Private/deleted exams are excluded from catalog and purchase.
- Payment reversal revokes linked store purchases in the same transaction as payment reversal and pass revocation. Its row is locked and status rechecked. Purchase-derived book/video access therefore cannot survive reversal.
- Unconfigured QPay requests are rejected before creating a Payment row, avoiding ghost pending invoices. Real callbacks continue to require provider verification; no provider success is claimed.
- Bank imports serialize by bank reference and atomically write bank row, automatic payment and linkage. Manual match locks the bank row and atomically writes its pending payment/linkage. Ignore cannot overwrite a completed match.
- Refund draft creation serializes by pupil/class and rejects repeated non-cancelled refunds for the same exit date. Submit/approve/paid/cancel updates include status predicates so concurrent decisions cannot overwrite terminal PAID/CANCELLED status. These are ledger transitions, not bank transfers.

- Global financial reads and delegated TEACHER_PLUS money operations require `finance.manage` granted to a stable user ID by ADMIN. Current role, grant expiry and revocation are checked on every request. No existing staff member is automatically granted it. Student/parent own views retain their narrow scopes.

## Remaining boundaries

Receipt creation now supports a stable UUID v4 clientRequestId per user, normalized body hash and database uniqueness. Ten simultaneous source requests produced one real PostgreSQL Payment; changed payload reuse returned 409, and another user’s same UUID did not share the row. Safe successful QR responses are persisted for replay. Before provider I/O, the invoice attempt is durably reserved: held or unknown acknowledgements return 503 on replay and never repeat the provider call. Stub-only tests confirmed exactly one invocation in both cases; zero external provider calls were made. The UUID is optional for legacy callers; callers that omit or rotate it cannot obtain this retry guarantee. Client receipt retention is device-local, not a durable cross-device ledger handoff. Generic receipt confirmation and store fulfillment remain separate calls; the store fulfillment transaction itself is atomic and safely retryable.

Actual QPay merchant setup and paid callback behavior remain unverified without provider credentials and authorized live testing. Confirmed store PASS fulfillment now creates UserPass and grants Purchase in the same transaction with settlement and per-user/pass locks. Pending PASS records grant nothing. Ten concurrent real PostgreSQL retries produced one seven-day UserPass, preserved the purchase price snapshot, and reversal revoked both entitlements. Pass ref/active state/duration are validated. Already-granted retries do not recreate a manually revoked UserPass; legacy already-granted PASS records without UserPass require explicit reconciliation. New pending PASS purchases freeze validated version-1 scope and duration terms in Purchase.passSnapshot; fulfillment preserves those terms in UserPass.scopeSnapshot. Actual PostgreSQL tests changed catalog scope/duration before confirmation and deactivated the catalog afterward: access and expiry kept the frozen terms. Legacy pending purchases without snapshots explicitly adopt current terms at fulfillment; their historical terms cannot be reconstructed. BOOK ref/video definition snapshots remain outside this round. Manual confirmation with a pass grant now shares a transaction: an inactive-pass failure rolled the payment back to PENDING, and simultaneous confirms produced one success and one entitlement. Staff grants share payment-row and user/pass locks, reject consumed settlement, freeze scope and require live finance permission. Explicit linked pass revocation clears its purchase grant and records an expired tombstone; same-payment retries cannot restore it, while a new valid receipt can purchase new terms. These synthetic ledger tests do not prove external cash receipt or transfers. Bank audit recording uses existing AuditService. No local test is presented as proof of a provider refund.

Refund calculation remains a business-model gap: preview currently expands only joinedOn through leftOn as the full lesson denominator and sums every confirmed payment for that user across classes/terms without allocation or prior-refund subtraction. A correct contract-end period and ledger allocation are required before certifying unused-term refund amounts. No contract period or monetary calculation was invented in this round.

Monthly cash report now uses confirmed Payment.amount once, based on paidAt in Ulaanbaatar [month start, next month start). Purchase.price never adds income. Granted BOOK/TEST associations classify the receipt; confirmed receipts with pending fulfillment or conflicting legacy product kinds go to other. Conflicting settled kinds also expose a reconciliation count. Pending/reversed payments and grants without a Payment do not contribute. Standalone confirmed manual receipts retain trainingFees classification. This is a cash receipt summary, not accrual accounting or certified net-after-refund reporting.

Validated store quotes use the frozen Purchase.price, including products below the legacy 1000 minimum; plain tuition creation retains that minimum and the student tuition cap. Quote-bound receipts cannot fund another product. ## Store administrator receipt summary

Purchase.price is reported separately as totalOrderValue; unfunded active quotes are pendingQuoteValue, and funded quotes awaiting entitlement are awaitingFulfillmentValue. Separate paid refunds remain unallocated, so netAfterSeparateRefunds is explicitly null rather than an invented net figure. The matching administrator UI labels order values and cash separately and does not infer cash from old price totals.
